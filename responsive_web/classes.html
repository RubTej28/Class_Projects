Classes Overview

Magic

The Magic class specializes in ranged magic attacks, allowing players to deal significant damage from a distance. Magic weapons consume an energy called mana which can be regenerated naturally overtime or through the use of certain items and accessories. Players begin the game with only 20 mana, but this can be increased to 200 through the use of mana crystals which can be easily crafted with 5 fallen stars. Players who enjoy using spells and maintaining a safe distance from enemies might find the Magic class appealing.

Melee

The Melee class focuses on close-range combat, using swords, axes, and other melee weapons to deal high damage to enemies. The melee class is typically the first class most players pick in their playthrough due to the high defense and how easy it is to get a melee weapon from the start. Players who like direct confrontation and high-risk, high-reward playstyles might enjoy the melee class.

Summoner

The Summoner class specializes in summoning minions to fight on their behalf. Summoner players can also use whips to tag enemies or bosses and deal extra damage. If optimized correctly, the summoner class, while fragile, can be one of the highest damage dealing classes in the game. Those who are good at dodging and have a more strategic, less direct approach to combat will find the Summoner class exciting

Ranger

The Ranger class excels at ranged physical attacks, using bows, guns, and other ranged weapons to eliminate enemies from a distance. Rangers also have access to various ammunition types, allowing them to adapt to different combat situations. Players who would rather maintaim a safe distance while dealing significant damage might find the Ranger class fun to play.

Sub-Classes

In addition to the main four classes, there also exists many sub-classes within each of them. Many players will try to beat the game with specific sub-classes for a more challenging experience. For example, The melee class has a variety of different boomerangs and yo-yo's that you can use throughout your playthrough. Since there aren't as many yo-yos and boomerangs as other melee weapons, players who want a more difficult and different experience can attempt to beat the game with only that sub-class.

The ranger class used to have an official sub-class called the thrower class. This class consisted of using various thrown weapons, such as knives, shurikens, and other throwable items. However, due to the lack of weapon variety, Re-Logic later decided to make those items a part of the general Ranger class rather than maintaining a separate sub-class.

Sub-classes admittadly become a little harder to pin down the when you get to the magic and summoner, due to the large array of different weapons for magic and smaller selection for summoner. Magic players can choose to attempt a run with only wands or spell tombs as their sub-class of choice. Since the summoner class really only consists of 2 weapon types, that being summoning staffs and whips, there really isn't a decernable sub-class for the summoner. If you really wanted to, you could try and beat the game with only whips but that would probably be miserable. Overall, the sub-classes in Terraria provide players with a variety of ways to customize their playstyle and challenge themselves in different ways.
